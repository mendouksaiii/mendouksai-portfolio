Mantle Sepolia · Chain 5003 · EIP-712

SENTINEL: AI-Signer Cryptographic Firewall

View Repository

In traditional agent setups, AI spending decisions are checked off-chain inside the agent process itself. If the process is hijacked or prompt-injected, the treasury is drained. SENTINEL decouples policy from execution: the agent proposes a transaction, an isolated evaluation node checks the plain-English policy rules, and signs a structured EIP-712 authorization. SentinelVault.sol enforces single-use nonces and deadline windows directly on Mantle Sepolia.

Redline Rider: 60 FPS Adaptive Quality Engine

Play Redline Rider

Achieving sustained 60 FPS in browser 3D without dedicated hardware requires continuous profiling. Redline Rider implements a rolling 60-frame moving average timer measuring CPU step and GPU render latency. If frame times exceed 21ms for 12 consecutive frames, shadow cascades drop from 2048px to 1024px and secondary terrain LOD steps down. Custom GLSL shaders use analytic math rather than texture lookups to keep bandwidth virtually zero.
